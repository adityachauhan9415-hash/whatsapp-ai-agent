# Problem Context

## The Problem
Maintaining meaningful WhatsApp conversations with many contacts while busy leads to late replies, missed social cues, and repetitive burnout.

## Why RAG vs Fine-tuning
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is used because it allows the agent to access up-to-date conversation history and factual data without the cost and rigidity of periodic fine-tuning. It keeps the model grounded in real facts.

## Role of the Persona Brain
The Persona Brain ensures consistency in the agent's tone, humor, and style based on your established preferences.

## Role of the History Brain
The History Brain tracks facts, previous topics, and shared context to provide continuity across multiple message threads.

## Memory Separation
Memory is separated by relationship type to ensure that personal secrets or specific social dynamics (e.g., family vs. work colleagues) are not leaked or applied cross-context.
